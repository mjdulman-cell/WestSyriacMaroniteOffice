Install this prayer book

On iPhone or iPad, open the app in Safari. Use Share, then Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if Safari offers it, then tap Add. Open the new icon while still online and wait for “Ready for offline reading.” On Android or desktop, use your browser’s Install or Add to Home Screen option when available.

How the app selects the day

Today follows your device’s civil date. All seven Ramsho translations identify the office as the eve of their named day, so for Saturday evening, select Sunday and open Ramsho. The Sootoro documents use weekday labels without a consistent civil-date rule. The app keeps their source assignments and leaves evening selection to you.

About the translations

The reader preserves the wording in the supplied English translations. Common prayers referred to by rubric and other known gaps are marked in the relevant offices. Preferences and reading positions are saved on this device.
